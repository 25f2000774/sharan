About

Teaching, research and engineering in practice

With 15 years of experience in higher education, I bring together academic leadership, research, project supervision, industry collaboration, and student mentoring. My work focuses on developing innovative engineering solutions, advancing interdisciplinary research, and fostering meaningful connections between academia and industry.

My research focuses on polymer and natural-fibre composites, sustainable materials, additive manufacturing, interfacial characterisation, circular-economy strategies, and the application of artificial intelligence to materials research. I have also completed a postdoctoral fellowship at Universitas Gadjah Mada (UGM), Indonesia, where I pursued collaborative research and strengthened my international academic engagement..

Research

Research interests

Research themes spanning materials performance, sustainable design, manufacturing, and computational approaches.

Polymer & Natural-Fibre Composites

Self-reinforced polymer composites, natural-fibre/epoxy systems, interfacial behaviour, and mechanical performance.

Sustainable Materials

Recycling, circular economy, sustainable biocomposites, and material choices for lower-impact engineering.

Surface Treatment & Characterization

Plasma treatment, combined surface modification, interface characterization, and composite property evaluation.

Additive Manufacturing

3D-printed auxetic and sandwich structures, lightweight design, and manufacturing-oriented innovation.

Computational Engineering

Finite element analysis and data-driven methods relevant to materials and mechanical engineering.

Engineering Education
